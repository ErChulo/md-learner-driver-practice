The recovered card reuses the already-local authentic R03-09B vector; the renderer produced the [recovery contact sheet](recovered-sign-contact-sheet.png). No manual artwork is redistributed.

The prior catalog and the 2026 Spanish manual were inspected. Federal 2024 release, 2004 edition, 2012 supplement / 2009 archive index, Commons category and Maryland SHA index were consulted as source inventories, not exhaustively searched per entry. The execution environment does not allow direct binary downloads from those hosts; no EPS conversion or external SVG acquisition is claimed. Existing local FHWA-derived SVGs were rendered in resvg and compared to manual pages. A code marked “variant” is an indicative family, not a claim that the example has been identified.

Failure classes: **1** incorrect mapping/orientation; **2** potential legacy federal design; **3** potentially Maryland-specific; **4** composite or customizable configuration; **5** licensing restriction; **6** other/unverified or non-sign diagram. These are working hypotheses, not proof of unavailability. All unresolved cards remain in the provenance registry but are excluded from the study stack.
